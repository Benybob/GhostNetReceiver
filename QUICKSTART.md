# GhostNet Receiver 0.4.2

1. Run **GhostNetReceiver-0.3.2-Setup.exe** or **Start.cmd**.
2. If SmartScreen appears: More info → Run anyway. Compare SHA-256 to `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
3. Choose your region. Click **Start listening**.
4. Watch the yellow banner for the frequency to tune (USB audio) or the online Kiwi frequency. **Hear audio** plays the receive stream on your speakers (headphones recommended for USB radio). It does not transmit.
5. For concurrent coverage, open **Advanced** and enable **Monitor all published regions at once**. The **Regional feeds** tab shows each lane; **All messages** combines them.
5. **Messages** shows GhostNet-tagged traffic first. Use Export GhostNet CSV after a net. **Net report** writes whether tagged traffic was heard.

JS8 is decoded. VARA, ALE, and voice are not. RTTY is a preview. No transmit.
